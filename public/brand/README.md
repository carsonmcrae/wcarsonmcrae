# Daysforth brand kit

Your life, as it unfolds.

Daysforth gives everyday moments and big milestones a place together, in the order they happened.

## Files

- `daysforth-lockup.svg` / `.png`: primary horizontal logo, blue mark and ink lettering.
- `daysforth-lockup-reversed.svg` / `.png`: blue mark and white lettering for dark backgrounds.
- `daysforth-lockup-mono.svg` / `.png`: one-color ink logo.
- `daysforth-stacked.svg` / `.png`: vertical lockup.
- `daysforth-wordmark.svg` / `.png`: lettering alone.
- `daysforth-mark.svg` / `.png`: app mark with a blue background.
- `daysforth-mark-mono.svg` and `daysforth-mark-white.svg`: transparent one-color marks.
- `daysforth-social.svg` / `.png`: 1200 × 630 social card.
- `daysforth-banner.svg` / `.png`: 1500 × 500 profile banner.
- `tokens.css`: reusable brand colors and typography values.
- `Inter-variable-latin.woff2`: self-hosted Latin variable font.
- `FONT-LICENSE.txt` and `ICON-LICENSE.txt`: third-party licenses.

SVG lettering is outlined, including the social templates. No font installation is needed to use
the vector originals. PNG logos have transparent backgrounds where appropriate.

## Logo use

The D connects three moments along one stem: an individual story continuing through time.

Leave at least half the mark’s width clear on every side. Keep the horizontal logo at least 128 px
wide. Use the mark alone at smaller sizes: 24 px minimum in an interface, 16 px for a favicon.

Use the supplied primary, reversed, or monochrome artwork. Preserve proportions and spacing.
Choose a variant with strong contrast. Keep the mark free of gradients, shadows, extra symbols,
and text. Write the product name as **Daysforth** and the domain as **daysforth.com**.

## Palette

| Name | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forth blue | #1746ff | Mark, links, selected controls, timeline points |
| Ink | #111318 | Wordmark, headings, body copy |
| Paper | #ffffff | Main canvas |
| Mist | #f5f7fb | Quiet secondary surfaces |
| Slate | #545e72 | Supporting copy |

Use white text on Forth blue. For dark interfaces, use #a9c0ff for active blue and #1d2126 for the
canvas. Text needs at least 4.5:1 contrast; meaningful control boundaries need at least 3:1.

## Typography

Inter. Body 400, controls 550, emphasis 650, headings 750–780. Body line height 1.5–1.6; compact
heading line height 1.25–1.35. Keep metadata, titles, captions, and actions visibly separated.

The wordmark was outlined from the official Inter 4.1 variable source. The web font is distributed
by Fontsource under the SIL Open Font License. The app uses Phosphor icons under the MIT license.

## Voice

Human, considered, and forward-looking. Use familiar words and contractions. Let the people and
photographs carry the personality. Label controls plainly: “Save,” “Back to the timeline,” and
“Go to date.”

Product line: **Your life, as it unfolds.**

Supporting line: **Give the whole story a place.**
